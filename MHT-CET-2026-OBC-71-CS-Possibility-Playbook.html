1   The Honest Headline

At 71 percentile, the Ladies/OBC quota will NOT get her CS/IT at a good college through CAP. CS is so over-subscribed that the Ladies-OBC (LOBC) cutoff is almost identical to the open cutoff — the quota barely helps for CS. Good-college CS/IT/AI-DS close at 95–99 percentile; she is far below that.

Her realistic plan (CAP + Spot only): (1) use CAP to lock the best free-tuition seat her rank can reach (a lower/mid college, ideally AI-DS/IT/ENTC); (2) use the spot / institute vacancy rounds after CAP-3 to try to upgrade to a slightly better college or branch from last-minute vacancies. Tuition stays ~₹0 via the 100% girls' waiver throughout.

4   SPOT / VACANCY ROUND — best colleges to focus on

After CAP-3, colleges fill vacant seats via institute-level / spot / "against-CAP" vacancy rounds (newspaper & college-website notices), strictly on inter-se merit, Maharashtra candidates first. This is her chance to upgrade from last-minute cancellations — usually in less-demanded branches or at lower-mid colleges, because good-college CS/IT almost never stays vacant.

7   Bottom Line

  • WORST (guaranteed): free-tuition bottom-tier CS/IT via CAP.
  • AVERAGE (most likely): free-tuition lower-mid college in IT/AI-DS via CAP.
  • BEST (realistic, luck-aided): a decent/mid college in ENTC/AI-DS/IT grabbed in a spot/vacancy round.
  • Not attainable: a top college in CS — cutoffs 95–99, and those seats never go vacant.
  • All outcomes keep tuition ~₹0 (100% girls' waiver). Spend the freed savings + effort on skills — that drives 2030 placement more than college tier.
