1   The Honest Headline

At 71 percentile, the Ladies/OBC quota will NOT get her CS/IT at a good college through CAP. CS is so over-subscribed that the Ladies-OBC (LOBC) cutoff is almost identical to the open cutoff — the quota barely helps for CS. Good-college CS/IT/AI-DS close at 95–99 percentile; she is far below that.

And with the corrected budget, paying her way into a top college is also out: top-college management quota is ₹10–14 lakh total, while she has ₹4–5 lakh total. So a top college in CS is unreachable both by merit AND by budget. Aiming there would only waste choices.
Her realistic, honest plan: (a) take a CAP seat at the best college her rank can reach — where the Maharashtra 100% girls' fee waiver makes tuition ₹0; and (b) spend the ₹4–5 lakh savings on a management seat ONLY if it buys a clearly better but affordable (≤ ₹1.25 lakh/yr) mid-tier college. Otherwise keep the savings for skills/living.

5   The three routes explained

Route A — CAP ladies/OBC quota (the main path now: free tuition)

  • Reliably gives a seat; at 71%ile it lands a lower-tier college for CS/IT (or ENTC/AI-DS at a slightly better lower college).
  • Key benefit: a CAP seat keeps the Maharashtra 100% girls' fee waiver + OBC scholarship → tuition ~₹0. With a tight total budget, this is now her most important lever.

Route B — Affordable management quota (only at cheap mid colleges)

  • ~20% of seats, filled by the college on CET score; no CAP cutoff barrier.
  • Only viable where total 4-yr fee ≤ ₹4–5 lakh (i.e., ~₹1–1.25 lakh/yr) — that means cheaper mid/lower colleges, NOT VIT/PCCOE/VIIT.
  • Note: a management seat forfeits the 100% waiver — so she'd pay from savings. Worth it only if the college is clearly better than her free CAP option.

Route C — Spot / Institute vacancy round (bonus lottery)

  • After CAP-3, colleges fill vacant seats via newspaper/website notices, on inter-se merit (Maharashtra candidates first).
  • Low-percentile students sometimes get seats from last-minute cancellations — but good-college CS/IT rarely stays vacant. Treat as a bonus, not a plan.
