1   The Honest Headline

At 71 percentile, the Ladies/OBC quota will NOT get her CS/IT at a good college through CAP. CS is so over-subscribed that the Ladies-OBC (LOBC) cutoff is almost identical to the open cutoff — the quota barely helps for CS. Good-college CS/IT/AI-DS close at 95–99 percentile; she is far below that.

But there is good news: she can spend ₹4–5 lakh/yr from savings. That makes the Management / Institute-level quota (which uses her CET score but has no CAP cutoff barrier) a realistic route to a good college with CS/IT/AI-DS. The strategy below runs CAP and management-quota in parallel.

5   The three routes explained

Route A — Management / Institute-level quota (her path to a GOOD college)

  • ~20% of seats, filled by the college directly using CET score; no CAP cutoff barrier.
  • Fees fit her ₹4–5L budget. Target: VIT Pune, PCCOE, VIIT, MIT Academy, AISSMS, DY Patil Akurdi for CS/IT/AI-DS.
  • Contact each college's admission office during CAP; apply with CET score; merit-based institute list.

Route B — Spot / Institute vacancy round (bonus lottery)

  • After CAP-3, colleges fill vacant seats via newspaper/website notices, on inter-se merit (Maharashtra candidates first).
  • Low-percentile students sometimes get seats from last-minute cancellations — but good-college CS/IT rarely stays vacant. Treat as a bonus, not a plan.

Route C — CAP ladies/OBC quota (the safety net)

  • Will reliably give a seat, but at 71%ile only a lower-tier college for CS/IT (or ENTC at a slightly better lower college).
  • Big upside: a CAP seat keeps her eligible for the Maharashtra 100% girls' fee waiver + OBC scholarship → tuition ~₹0 (management seats are excluded from this).
